Privacy

How Paperpulse uses your data

Paperpulse stores your email address, sign-in sessions, topic subscriptions, and proposals you submit. Passwordless sign-in tokens are stored only as hashes and expire after 15 minutes.

Topics, approved search terms, and generated reports are public and shared between readers. Your email address and personal subscription list are not public.

You can permanently remove your account from the account page. Proposals may remain as anonymized moderation records after deletion.
